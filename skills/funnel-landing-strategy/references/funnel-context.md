# Funnel context

Use this resource to gather and retain the context needed for the customer's Story and the 4 Cs. The document stores project knowledge; its headings are not sections of the final content.

## Create and reuse the document

For a new funnel project, create a Markdown context document in the project directory, using the user's location if specified; otherwise name it funnel-context.md. Reuse an existing relevant document rather than starting again. Update it when the audience, offer or supporting information changes. A small edit can use existing context without another discovery exercise.

Use the conversation, product material and available audience research first. Ask for missing information when it changes the message. Keep project-specific context outside the installed skill.

## What to record

Organize the document around these topics, with detail proportionate to the project:

### Project and offer

What is being offered, how it works, its capabilities and terms. What the requested content should accomplish and what action follows it.

### Identify

Who the potential customers are, the situation they are in, what they are trying to do and what problems they encounter. Record current approaches, alternatives and relevant constraints. Include audience language or sources when available.

### Outcome

List the results or experiences these people want, why they matter and what a useful before/after would look like.

### Objection: what they want to avoid

List unwanted experiences, consequences or tradeoffs while pursuing those outcomes: discomfort, wasted work, disruption, complexity, loss of control or another relevant downside. Connect each concern to the audience's situation and the desired outcome.

If people also express a buying hesitation, retain it in context. Avoid reducing this topic to doubts about the seller or to a generic FAQ.

### Supporting material

Demonstrations, outputs, customer experiences, credentials or results that can build trust.
