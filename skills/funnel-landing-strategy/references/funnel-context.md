# Record the project context

Keep what you learn about the customer and offer in a Markdown document. Use it to write the Story and build the funnel. Its headings organize your notes; they do not set the sections of the finished page or content.

## Create and reuse the document

For a new project, create funnel-context.md in the project directory, or use the name and location the user requests. If a relevant document already exists, use it. Update it when the audience, offer or other project information changes. For a small edit, use what is already recorded.

Start with the conversation, product information and available audience research. Ask for missing information when the answer would change the message. Keep the project document in the project, separate from the installed skill.

## What to write down

### Project and offer

What is being sold or offered? What does it do, and how does it work? Record its features, price and terms. Explain what the requested content should achieve and what you want the reader to do next.

### Identify

Who are the potential customers? What are they trying to do? How do they do it today, and what problems do they run into? Record other approaches they use and anything that affects their choices. Include their own words and relevant sources when available.

### Outcome

What results or experiences do these people want? List them and explain why they matter. Describe what would change in their work or life if they got that result.

### Objection: what they want to avoid

Look at how the customer tries to reach those results today, before introducing the product. What do they want to avoid? Record the problem, when it happens and how it affects them. It could be discomfort, repetitive work, delays, wasted money or another problem in their current approach.

Keep questions about buying or adopting the product separate. Learning new software or moving data to it are questions about the offer. The Story's Objection comes from the customer's current way of pursuing their goal.

### Material that can help build trust

Record available demonstrations, example outputs, customer experiences, credentials and results. Include enough detail to use them in the content later.
