# Review and measurement

Read for diagnosis, prioritization, measurement design or a quality pass. Inspect available evidence and state what cannot be assessed.

| Dimension | Review question | Consequential gap example |
|---|---|---|
| Path | Does each step resolve a real uncertainty? | Required email capture before a straightforward purchase. |
| Relevance | Is the intended situation recognizable? | A broad promise without the actual use case. |
| Continuity | Is the promise preserved through delivery? | A checklist ad leads to a page selling only a course. |
| Explanation | Are outcome and mechanism understandable? | Features with no connection to the task. |
| Evidence | Do materials support the specific claims? | Logos implying undocumented results. |
| Objections | Are consequential doubts answered? | Missing dimensions for a constrained space. |
| Offer | Are scope, terms, effort and fulfillment clear? | A demo without an agenda or purpose. |
| Action | Does it lead to the promised completed result? | Counting a click despite no confirmed booking. |
| Delivery | Is the original value supplied? | Resource withheld behind an unexpected upsell. |
| Measurement | Are local actions connected to outcomes? | Downloads measured while purchases are ignored. |

Map the 4 Cs for coverage. A missing section titled “Convince” is not a defect; missing information required for confidence can be.

## Findings

For each issue report location or behavior, affected decision, proposed change, evidence and uncertainty. Prioritize by decision impact and dependencies. Distinguish functional problems from performance hypotheses. Avoid numerical scores without a defined basis. A screenshot cannot establish link behavior, delivery or commercial performance.

## Path-specific observations

| Path | Local and intermediate observations | Downstream outcomes |
|---|---|---|
| Direct sale | Evaluation, cart, checkout and completed purchases. | Fulfillment, returns and dissatisfaction reasons. |
| Resource | Valid requests, delivery, useful application where observable, paid-offer visits. | Purchases by contact cohort, feedback and unsubscribe signals. |
| Demo | Confirmed bookings, attendance, fit assessment and qualified opportunities. | Proposals and paid activations, including non-fit reasons. |
| Trial | Activation and meaningful use related to the promised value. | Paid adoption and continued use where relevant. |

Define numerator, denominator and counting unit. Orders per session differ from customers per unique visitor. For delayed results specify cohort, observation window and attribution, and note incomplete observation. Explain qualification rather than using an undefined label.

No universal conversion benchmark or email contribution applies. Segment by relevant intent and traffic. Clicks and dwell time are not direct measures of curiosity or trust. A drop-off locates an investigation but does not prove its cause.

## Match the verification to the question

- **Conceptual review:** does context support path, message, evidence and action? No performance conclusion follows.
- **Comprehension check:** can intended users explain offer, audience, suitability and next action?
- **Behavioral observation:** can people find needed information and complete the action?
- **Commercial experiment:** does a change improve the relevant outcome under comparable conditions? Choose an appropriate sample and duration and state limitations.

Record the change, rationale, main outcome and relevant downstream consequences. Hypothetical walkthroughs are not A/B tests; simulated examples do not prove that a funnel will convert.
