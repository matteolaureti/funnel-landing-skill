# Three hypothetical walkthroughs

Read for illustrations. Products, capabilities, prices and terms are assumptions. These examples support conceptual review; no live performance or customer evidence has been established. They are not compulsory templates.

## Direct sale: desk organizer

**Context:** people working from home on small desks have scattered cables and accessories. Entry content shows the objects being organized. Hypothetical product: removable compartments, €39 product price. Dimensions, shipping and return terms are missing.

**Path:** content → product landing → checkout → confirmation. Fit can be assessed autonomously when practical details are supplied. A required resource does not resolve an identified uncertainty.

**4 Cs:** familiar clutter earns attention; organized objects create interest; scale images, configurations and terms establish fit; clear selection leads to purchase.

**Hook:** “Give cables and accessories a place on your desk.”

**Story:** identify the small-desk situation; show accessible, collected accessories; explain compartments; address size, capacity, materials and commercial conditions. Do not invent productivity improvements.

**Offer and action:** actual product, variant, price and terms. “Choose your organizer” starts variant selection; “Buy” is appropriate once selection is complete.

**Page:** real photo and price; configurations; mechanism; dimensions and limits; commercial answers; consistent action. Checkout preserves selection and clarifies total cost. Confirmation explains the order and delivery.

**Evidence gaps:** actual images, measurements, specifications and commercial terms. The hypothetical plan can proceed; public claims need verification.

**Measurement:** completed purchases and checkout stages, plus returns and dissatisfaction. Observe whether dimensions and scale images help evaluation; button clicks alone do not establish more suitable purchases.

**Changed condition:** custom dimensions requiring a quotation justify assessment before purchase. Physical product type alone did not select the original path.

## Resource: freelancer's quotation checklist

**Context:** freelancers need to review scope, revisions, timing and conditions before sending quotations. Entry is educational content. Hypothetical paid offer: €89 course with proposal examples and editable models.

**Path:** content → checklist page → access and delivery → relevant follow-up when requested → course page → checkout → course access. The checklist addresses the same task as the paid offer and is useful on its own.

**4 Cs:** a familiar quotation doubt earns attention; an example creates interest; preview and actual use establish usefulness; resource request is a local conversion. The course needs its own explanation, evidence and purchase action.

**Hook:** “Before sending your next quotation, check what you are promising.”

**Story:** identify the quotation task; offer concrete review checks; demonstrate one completed check; answer concerns about generic content, fit and later communications. Do not guarantee more clients or fewer disputes.

**Entry offer and action:** complete checklist with stated delivery; “Receive the checklist.” Request useful delivery information and explain actual subsequent communications. Resource acquisition is not permission for unrelated promotion.

**Pages:** specific hero, readable preview, use and limits, contents and form. Confirmation supplies access or clear delivery instructions. Optional follow-up demonstrates use and explains the paid next step. The course page clarifies additional learning, models, effort, price and access.

**Evidence gaps:** actual resource, preview, course material and communication flow. An author profile alone does not demonstrate usefulness.

**Measurement:** valid requests, delivery, relevant use signals, paid-offer visits and purchases per cohort within a stated window. More contacts do not automatically mean more customers.

**Changed condition:** a person specifically seeking the course can reach its page directly; the checklist becomes an optional aid.

## Demo: software for training centers

**Context:** operational managers coordinate courses, instructors, calendars and confirmations across locations. Fit requires contextual evaluation and potentially other stakeholders. Capabilities are hypothetical; integrations and import functions are unconfirmed.

**Path:** targeted entry → demo landing → booking → confirmation → demo → suitable proposal → paid activation. A demonstration addresses workflow uncertainty.

**4 Cs:** coordination difficulties earn attention; a relevant flow creates interest; actual screens, limits and demonstration establish applicability; slot confirmation is the local conversion and activation is the final one.

**Hook:** “Coordinating courses and instructors across locations? See how bookings and confirmations can work in one flow.”

**Story:** identify the operational role; show the supported process instead of numerical savings; explain actual behavior; address locations, roles, existing data, setup, cost and meeting content.

**Entry offer and action:** hypothetical free 25-minute product demo with agenda; “Book a demo.” It is not independent consulting.

**Pages:** relevant workflow, real screens, capabilities, agenda, doubts and booking. Fields support preparation or justified qualification. Confirmation gives slot, time zone, link, preparation and rescheduling. The demo fulfills its agenda; a proposal reflects verified needs, limitations, price and setup.

**Evidence gaps:** working demonstration, configurations, integrations, onboarding and commercial terms. Missing testimonials can be addressed with observable functions; unsupported integrations cannot.

**Measurement:** bookings, attendance, defined qualification, proposals and paid activations. More meetings can coexist with worse fit.

**Changed condition:** meaningful autonomous evaluation supports a trial with optional demo help; reconsider a mandatory meeting.

## Interpretation

The same method yields different paths, evidence and commitments while preserving promise and delivery. This is conceptual consistency, not a proven conversion improvement or an independent agent evaluation.
